# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One person, or a small creative team, who thinks visually: designers, communicators, marketers, developers and students who sketch diagrams, mind maps, wireframes and plans. They work on a Windows desktop, often for hours, switching between Aven, a notes app (Obsidian or Notion) and cloud storage (Google Drive). The job: get an idea out of the head and onto a canvas fast, keep the result organised by project, and drop it into the rest of their tools without friction.

## Product Purpose
Aven is a local-first desktop visual workspace: an Excalidraw-class infinite canvas wrapped in a real project manager (workspaces, projects, folders, templates, backups) with first-class exports to PNG, SVG, PDF, Obsidian, Notion and Google Drive. Success means drawings never get lost, never end up in one messy folder, and reach the place they are needed in two clicks.

## Brand Personality
Creative, clear, light on its feet. The "Envol" origami bird says ideas take flight; the interface stays calm so the user's drawings carry the colour. Voice: plain, specific verbs ("Export to vault", "Restore backup"), no hype.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero metrics, identical card grids, gradient text.
- Excalidraw's own branding and hand-drawn UI chrome (we reuse its open-source engine, not its identity).
- Heavy glassmorphism, neon gradients, decorative motion.
- Corporate monochrome marks (the user explicitly rejected a "looks like any company" logo).

## Design Principles
1. The canvas is the hero: chrome recedes, colour belongs to the user's work.
2. Nothing is ever lost: every destructive action is undoable or recoverable, and save state is always visible.
3. Earned familiarity: Excalidraw users and Windows users should feel at home in seconds; standard shortcuts, context menus, keyboard access.
4. Organised by default: every drawing has a home (workspace, project, folder) without the user filing it manually.
5. Honest features: no button without behaviour; integrations say exactly what they do and do not do.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA for UI text and controls (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI components). Full keyboard operation of the shell, visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, colour never the only signal (icons + labels on states).
